# Job Matching Platform

A comprehensive job matching platform designed to connect job seekers with employment opportunities, helping fight poverty through accessible employment access.

## Mission

Empower individuals from underprivileged backgrounds to find meaningful employment and build sustainable livelihoods through intelligent job matching.

## Features (Planned)

- **User Registration & Profiles**: Job seekers create profiles with skills and experience
- **Job Listings**: Employers post job opportunities
- **Intelligent Matching Algorithm**: Match job seekers with suitable positions
- **Application Management**: Track job applications and status
- **Employer Dashboard**: Manage job postings and candidates
- **Search & Filtering**: Advanced search capabilities for jobs
- **Notifications**: Real-time updates on matches and applications
